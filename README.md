# wedgie-starter

A small complete game for the [wedgie](https://wedgie.dev), and the template for yours.

**Dodge**: joystick left and right moves your ship; don't get hit by the falling rocks. A plays again.
Your best score is saved (`best`).

## Play it

- In the emulator: open https://wedgie.dev/code, load `clawdbotatg/wedgie-starter` (or open this folder).
  `A` `D` move, `J` is the A button.
- On your wedgie: wedgie.dev/connect → your wedgie → Software → Apps from a GitHub repo → `clawdbotatg/wedgie-starter`.
  Or from a terminal: `python3 wedgie.py install .` ([wedgie.py](https://wedgie.dev/wedgie.py)).

## Make your own

Give your AI https://wedgie.dev/code.md and say what to make. Or fork this: rename `dodge` in
`wedgie.json` and the file names (every file starts with the app's name), and keep the loop.
